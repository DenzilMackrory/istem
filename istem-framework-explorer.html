What it is

A tool for teachers to assess their own courses, rating course and unit planning against the seven iSTEM criteria, from Beginning to Mastery. It is designed to help you improve your course through self-evaluation.

Why use it

What gets measured gets improved. Rating your unit each semester shows where your planning is strong, where it needs work, and how far it has come since your last evaluation.

iSTEM Teacher Assessment Tool V2. Developed by STEM Innovation Academy using Roehrig et al. (2021) and Dare et al. (2021).
